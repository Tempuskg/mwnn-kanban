## Description
Week 4 (T9). Compare each existing counter with the baseline, and note which referrers and threads produced installs, issues, or conversation. Apply the keep-or-drop rule: a recurring tactic stays if, across two consecutive monthly reviews, it shows any attributable signal or costs no more than 15 minutes a week.
